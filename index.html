03 / WHAT CHANGES, WHAT STAYS

Generated within
meaningful constraints.

The implementation is a bounded, graph-driven generator with real action-dependent feedback. Its scope is explicit.

IFIXED BY THE TASK DEFINITION

The rules of the world.

Six demand definitions, supported families, lifecycle rules, action scopes, scoring, and reserved schedules or attempt windows.

IIGENERATED BEFORE EXECUTION

The particular experience.

Seeded preferences and target values, world and historical records, controlled public text, and permitted independent orderings.

IIIDETERMINED DURING EXECUTION

What actions change.

Actual device writes, learning progress, corrections and confirmations, the next unfinished field, violations and outcomes.

THE CURRENT BOUNDARY

The engine does not yet simulate an unrestricted user life or invent arbitrary new goals in response to actions. D6 can reuse D1 or D5 mechanisms; this is not yet an arbitrary scene mixing all six demands.
